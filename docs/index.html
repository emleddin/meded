Medical Education Resources

About

Welcome! This website is basically my reference guide for medical education. It grew out of a series of Google files I created and shared over the course of medical school. I realized very quickly that some of my tables, like the one for medical abbreviations, would be really useful if they were accessible to more than just my friends. As you rotate through the hospital, you’ll find that each specialty (and subspecialty, and subsubspecialty) has its own set of acronyms. Plus, in the hospital setting, people tend to talk in brand names (the horror!). If you made it here, I hope you find it useful.
