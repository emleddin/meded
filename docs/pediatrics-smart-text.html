Safe Sleep
- 

Environment
- Make sure there are working smoke alarms and carbon monoxide alarms in the home

Feeding
- No honey or cow's milk until 1 year
- If using formula, mix one leveled-off scoop of formula to every 2 oz of water
- For babies only recieving breast milk, supplement with Poly-Vi-Sol daily
- Breastfeeding: Feed every 1 to 3 hours during the day and every 3 hours during the night for 8 to 12 feedings per day. Give baby vitamin D daily (400 IU per day).

Smoking
- Smoking around babies and young children can lead to more frequent illnesses, asthma, and sudden infant death syndrome (SIDS)
- Avoid smoking in the home. Any smoking outside the home should change their clothes and wash their hands before interacting with the baby.
